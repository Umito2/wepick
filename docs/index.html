A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

Good questions.
Easy answers.

What is the EGP budget based on?+

These are illustrative spending tiers for two people: up to EGP 300, EGP 301–700, and over EGP 700. Choose “Up to EGP 700” to include the first two tiers, or “Any budget” to see all ideas. The demo uses preloaded ideas and does not fetch live menu prices. Confirm current costs with the restaurant or venue.

How do we connect with each other?+

In the Android app, one partner creates a six-character invite code. The other enters it to join the same couple room. Once connected, either partner can start a shared round.

Is the website demo connected to my partner?+

The website demo runs in this browser. Switch between the two turns to try both partners’ choices. Real partner pairing and device sync are part of the Android app and require its Firebase backend.

Can I install the Android preview on my phone?+

You can install it, but the supplied developer preview cannot connect to its backend from a real phone as configured. It points to 10.0.2.2 for a computer Android emulator and requires the local Firebase Auth, Firestore, and Functions emulators. For a phone, rebuild the source with a reachable emulator host or configure a production Firebase project. The browser demo works without that setup.

Why is the Android preview stuck connecting?+

The preview needs its local Firebase backend to be running and reachable. Version 1.0.2 adds a bounded initial connection wait and retry controls. After starting the backend, retry the connection, or use Try browser demo to try both turns in your browser. A valid invite waiting for your partner continues listening normally.

Can I use Google to sign in?+

The emulator preview uses anonymous sign-in. Google sign-in is available in the production build after Firebase OAuth and App Check are configured.

What happens if neither of us likes anything?+

If there are no remaining likes, start a new round with a different mood or category. The tie-breaker only chooses from ideas at least one partner liked, and it always excludes vetoed items.
